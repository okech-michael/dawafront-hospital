Outpatient Care

Our outpatient department handles a wide variety of medical conditions for patients who do not require hospital admission. You'll receive a thorough examination, diagnosis, and treatment plan from our experienced physicians. Follow-up appointments are scheduled as needed.

General Consultation
Chronic Disease Management

Inpatient Care

For patients who require close monitoring and continuous treatment, our inpatient ward offers round-the-clock care by qualified nurses and physicians. We provide comfortable rooms, nutritional support, and regular medical reviews to ensure full recovery.

24/7 Nursing
Daily Doctor Rounds
In-room Meals

Pharmacy & Surgical Supplies

Our pharmacy is stocked with a wide range of prescription and over-the-counter medications, as well as surgical and medical consumables. Our qualified pharmacists provide guidance on medication use, side effects, and drug interactions. We also offer a drug delivery service for patients who cannot visit the clinic.

Prescription Medications
Surgical Consumables
Drug Delivery

Laboratory Services

Our on-site laboratory provides a comprehensive range of diagnostic tests including full blood count, blood glucose, malaria RDT, HIV testing, urinalysis, cultures, liver and kidney function tests, and more. Most results are available same-day to ensure timely clinical decisions.

Blood Tests
Malaria & HIV Screening
Same-Day Results

Theatre (Surgery)

Our fully equipped operating theatre supports a range of elective and emergency surgical procedures. Our skilled surgical team handles everything from minor operations to more complex procedures, with comprehensive pre-operative assessment, anaesthesia, and post-operative care provided throughout.

Elective Surgery
Emergency Procedures
Post-op Care

Maternity Services

We provide comprehensive maternity care from antenatal clinics through to safe delivery and postnatal follow-up. Our maternity ward is staffed by dedicated midwives and obstetricians who ensure the safety and comfort of both mother and baby at every stage.

Antenatal Clinics
Safe Delivery

MCH / Family Planning
